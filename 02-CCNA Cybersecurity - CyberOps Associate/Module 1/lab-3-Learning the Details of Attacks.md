## Objective

The goal of this lab was to research and analyze vulnerabilities in Internet of Things (IoT) applications, and understand how these weaknesses can be exploited in real-world scenarios.

---

## 🔍 Selected Vulnerability: Insecure Smart Camera (IoT Device)

### a. What is the vulnerability?

The vulnerability is the use of **default or weak credentials** in IoT devices such as smart cameras.

Many of these devices are shipped with default usernames and passwords that users either don’t change or are unaware of. This makes it easy for attackers to gain unauthorized access.

---

### b. Who might exploit it?

This vulnerability can be exploited by:

- Cybercriminals looking to spy on users  
- Hackers building botnets for larger attacks (e.g., DDoS)  
- Individuals with malicious intent who want unauthorized surveillance  

These attackers take advantage of weak security configurations to gain control of the device.

---

### c. Why does the vulnerability exist?

This vulnerability exists mainly because:

- Manufacturers prioritize ease of use over security  
- Devices are shipped with default credentials  
- Users are often unaware of the need to update or secure their devices  
- Lack of regular firmware updates  

This shows that both design decisions and user behavior contribute to the problem.

---

### d. What could be done to limit the vulnerability?

To reduce this risk, the following measures can be taken:

- Enforcing password changes during initial setup  
- Using strong, unique passwords  
- Regular firmware updates and patches  
- Disabling unnecessary remote access  
- Improving security standards during device manufacturing

## What I Learned

This lab helped me understand that:

- IoT devices are one of the weakest points in modern networks  
- Many vulnerabilities exist because security is treated as an afterthought  
- Simple issues like default passwords can lead to serious breaches

IoT devices may look simple, but they can create serious security risks if not properly managed.

This lab made me realize that security is not just about complex systems, sometimes the biggest problems come from basic oversights.
